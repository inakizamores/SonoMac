## SonoMac 1.2 — V1 complete

The polished, complete V1 of SonoMac: native Sonos control living in your menu bar.

**Highlights**
- 🎚️ Whole-house master volume, per-room and per-speaker bars, one-click **Even Out Volumes**
- 🔗 Grouping: merge rooms, separate speakers, party mode
- ⏯️ Playback and **TV Audio** switching for home-theater rooms
- 🎛️ Per-room sound settings: bass, treble, loudness, sub, surrounds, Night Sound, Speech Enhancement
- 🖱️ Damped swipe-to-adjust on every bar; drift-proof group volume
- ⚡ Instant panel, zero background cost, no cloud, no account, no dependencies

**Install:** download `SonoMac.dmg` below, drag to Applications, launch.
First launch: macOS will warn that the app isn't notarized (it's open source, unsigned) —
go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**. Full instructions and a
one-line Terminal install that skips the dialog are in the
[README](https://github.com/inakizamores/SonoMac#install).

**Requires:** macOS 14+, Apple Silicon, a Sonos S2 system on your network.
